\## 1. Information we collect



\### 1a. Information you provide



\- \*\*Email address\*\* and \*\*password\*\* when you create an account.

\- \*\*Display name\*\* you set during sign-up.

\- \*\*Username\*\* (optional, public) used for friend search and on the

&#x20; leaderboard.

\- \*\*Profile avatar\*\* — either a built-in icon you select, or a photo

&#x20; you pick from your device's gallery (PRO subscribers only). Photos

&#x20; picked from the gallery are stored only on your device; the file

&#x20; path is saved to your profile but the image itself is never

&#x20; uploaded to our servers or shared with other users.

\- \*\*Clan name\*\* if you create or join a clan.

\- \*\*Chat messages\*\* you send in 1-on-1 friend chats or clan chats.



\### 1b. Information collected automatically



\- \*\*Firebase Authentication User ID\*\* — a randomly generated

&#x20; identifier that links your profile across devices.

\- \*\*Android device identifier (`Settings.Secure.ANDROID\_ID`)\*\* — used

&#x20; to bind your trial / PRO activation status to your specific device

&#x20; so it survives reinstalls. Not used for advertising, profiling, or

&#x20; cross-app tracking.

\- \*\*Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token\*\* — a per-install

&#x20; identifier that lets the operating system deliver our push

&#x20; notifications. Mirrored to your user profile so our notification

&#x20; backend can target you. Rotated by the OS.

\- \*\*Session token\*\* — a random UUID generated when you sign in, used

&#x20; to enforce the "one active device at a time" rule.

\- \*\*Crash diagnostics\*\* — when the App crashes we send a stack

&#x20; trace, your device model, and your Android version to Firebase

&#x20; Crashlytics so we can identify and fix bugs. We do not attach your

&#x20; email, name, or in-app content to crash reports.

\- \*\*Gameplay activity\*\* — your XP, coins, current streak, lesson

&#x20; progress, daily quest completion, and (PRO only) your mistake

&#x20; history. Stored in your profile so progress syncs across devices.



\### 1c. Permissions you may grant



These are off by default. The App only asks when you actively use

the feature that needs them:



\- \*\*Microphone\*\* — requested when you start the "Pronunciation"

&#x20; phase of a lesson. Audio is processed by your device's built-in

&#x20; speech recogniser (provided by Android); we receive only the

&#x20; resulting text transcript, never the audio recording itself.

\- \*\*Photo library access\*\* — requested only when you tap "Pick from

&#x20; gallery" on the avatar selection screen (PRO feature). We use

&#x20; Android's system photo picker, which gives us access only to the

&#x20; single photo you select — never your full library.

\- \*\*Notifications\*\* — requested so we can deliver friend requests,

&#x20; new clan / private messages, and daily streak reminders.



You can revoke any of these at any time from your device's system

settings (Settings → Apps → Mandarin Master → Permissions).

\- To create and authenticate your account.

\- To save and sync your learning progress across devices.

\- To deliver the social features you opt into (friends list, clan

&#x20; membership, in-app chat, leaderboard).

\- To send notifications you've allowed (friend requests, new

&#x20; messages, streak reminders).

\- To diagnose crashes and improve the App.

\- To enforce trial limits and the single-device sign-in rule

&#x20; (anti-abuse / fraud prevention).

\- To respond to your support requests.



\## 3. Who we share your information with



We share data only with service providers that are necessary to run

the App. We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone.



| Provider | What we share | Why | Where stored |

|---|---|---|---|

| Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, FCM, Crashlytics) | Account data, gameplay data, push tokens, crash reports | Backend infrastructure | Google data centres (primarily US, with global replication for FCM) |

| Render Inc. | Read-only access to the same Firebase data | Listens for new messages and dispatches push notifications | US data centres |

| jsDelivr CDN | Your IP address (standard HTTP) | Fetches Chinese character stroke-order data on first use of each character; cached locally afterwards | Global CDN |

| Google Play (for paid subscribers) | Your Play purchase token | Verifies and renews your subscription | Google data centres |



Other clan members and friends you've accepted will see your public

username, display name, avatar, total XP, current streak, and any

messages you send in shared chats.



\## 4. How long we keep your data



\- \*\*Active account data\*\* — as long as your account exists.

\- \*\*Clan chat messages\*\* — only the most recent 200 messages per

&#x20; clan are kept; older messages are auto-deleted.

\- \*\*Private chat messages\*\* — only the most recent 100 messages per

&#x20; conversation are kept, and messages older than 3 days are filtered

&#x20; out client-side.

\- \*\*Crash reports\*\* — retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90

&#x20; days, then automatically deleted.

\- \*\*After you delete your account\*\* (see section 6) — all profile

&#x20; data, social graph, chat messages tied to your account, and trial

&#x20; / subscription state are removed from our database immediately.



\## 5. Your rights



You can:



\- \*\*Access your data\*\* by viewing your profile inside the App.

\- \*\*Correct your name / username / avatar\*\* from the in-app

&#x20; Settings → Manage account screen.

\- \*\*Delete your account\*\* at any time inside the App

&#x20; (Settings → Manage account → Delete account), or by email if you

&#x20; cannot sign in (see section 6 below).

\- \*\*Withdraw permissions\*\* (microphone, notifications) via your

&#x20; device's system settings.



If you are in the European Union, United Kingdom, or another GDPR

jurisdiction, you additionally have the right to data portability

and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection

authority.



If you are a resident of California, the CCPA gives you the rights

to know, delete, and not be discriminated against for exercising

your privacy rights. We do not "sell" personal information as

defined by the CCPA.



\## 6. Account deletion



You can delete your Mandarin Master account at any time. Two paths:



\### Inside the App (recommended)



1\. Sign in.

2\. Open Settings → Manage account.

3\. Tap "Delete account" and confirm. You may be asked to re-enter

&#x20;  your password (a Firebase security requirement).



\### Without signing in



If you cannot sign in (forgot password, lost device), email us at

xab8101@gmail.com with the subject line "Mandarin Master account

deletion" and the email address on the account. We will verify the

request and complete the deletion within 30 days, and send you an

email confirmation when it's done.



Once your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:



\- Your profile (name, username, email, avatar, XP, coins, streak,

&#x20; energy, lives)

\- Your social graph (friends, friend requests, clan membership)

\- Chat messages you sent (note: messages you sent that other

&#x20; participants quoted or replied to may remain in their copies of

&#x20; the conversation, since we cannot edit other users' chat history)

\- Trial credits and subscription state

\- Mistake history (PRO feature)



The following may persist:



\- Anonymised crash reports in Firebase Crashlytics (no personal

&#x20; identifiers attached; auto-deleted after 90 days)
